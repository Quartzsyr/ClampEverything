No glass ribbons, no floating abstract shapes, no mountains, no scenery, no ornamental folds across the middle, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no mockup screen, no interface, no buttons, no border. Truly minimal wallpaper with a project-relevant binder clip, not an illustration of a web page.
